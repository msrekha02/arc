The unconstrained arm contacts everyone every cycle and burns the population down doing it: its recovery decays as the response model's annoyance term bites, and by the fourth cycle it is collecting less than the constrained arm it started ahead of. That crossover is the argument for the constraints, and it is why beating it on net value is a result rather than an accident.
